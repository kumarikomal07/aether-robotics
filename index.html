Research & innovation

Exploring what comes next in autonomy.

Our applied research explores emerging methods in machine learning, perception and human-robot collaboration, and tests whether they are ready for real-world use.

  • Machine Learning & Deep Learning

    Exploring efficient training, small-data learning and model compression so advanced models can run reliably on industrial hardware.

  • Neural Networks & Intelligent Systems

    Studying architectures that combine learned models with rules and planning to make systems more dependable and explainable.

  • Autonomous Robotics

    Investigating perception, planning and control methods that let robots operate safely in changing, human-shared environments.

  • Computer Vision Research

    Evaluating detection, segmentation and 3D perception methods for inspection and manipulation tasks.

Our development process

From discovery to continuous optimisation.

A structured, five-stage engineering process that keeps risk low and value visible at every step.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand goals, constraints, data and the operational environment.

  2. 02

    Design

    Architect the AI, robotics and integration approach, and simulate it.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Build models, software and robot cells in iterative, tested sprints.

  4. 04

    Integrate

    Connect with existing systems, train teams and validate safety.

  5. 05

    Optimize

    Monitor performance, retrain models and improve continuously.
