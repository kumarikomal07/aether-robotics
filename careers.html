Company culture

Curious minds, engineering discipline.

We value clear thinking, careful engineering and kindness. Ideas are judged on evidence, not hierarchy.

Our engineering and innovation environment combines a hands-on robotics lab, simulation tools and modern MLOps infrastructure, so you can move from idea to experiment quickly and safely.

To confirm before launch: Benefits, working arrangements and lab facilities to be confirmed by HR.

Why work with Aether Robotics?

Reasons engineers join us.

  • Real-world impact

    Your work leaves the lab and runs on factory floors, warehouses and hospitals.

  • Multidisciplinary team

    Work daily with ML, robotics, embedded and software engineers.

  • Time to learn

    Dedicated learning time, conference budget and internal tech talks.

  • Responsible engineering

    Safety, ethics and honesty are part of how we build.
